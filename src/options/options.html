Guard

Before every send, the guard checks that the message cannot reveal your real address. From a real address you can only write to reverse aliases and to the addresses allowed below. From an alias, your real address in the recipients, subject, text or attachments blocks the message. A blocked message stays open, and the Alias Buddy button in the compose window says why.
